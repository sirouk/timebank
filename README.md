# Parent-Child Behavioral Free Time Rewards Time System

## Description:
Kids like to spend time on electronics and video games. Other kids love to get outside, roam, discover, and play. That’s fine as long as they do so in a balanced fashion that is healthy but still allows them to socialize and get a break from their responsibilities.

What would be better is a system where kids can earn free time and learn how to manage their risks and rewards.

Therefore, we have come up with a system to reward good behavior and well managed risk. It should easy enough to follow along!


## Parameters:
- A cap in the number of hours per week on selected free time activity
    - Two hours
- Acceptable activities for free time determined by parent
    - computer or video game console
- Day(s) of the week available to Child for time utilization
    - Friday/Saturday
- Day of the week when Parent and Child settle on previous week and plan the next
    - Saturday/Sunday
- Ask first for permission to utilize and start any session 
- All daily responsibilities must be done before requesting time utilization
    - chores, homework, projects
- Starting balance set and provided by parents depending on optimal parameters
    - TBD
- Parents determine value of time units
    - $1 per 10 minutes
- Maximum daily penalty in the form of reduced collateral determined by parents
    - $1
- Parents determine behavior that is eligible for reducing collateral
    - not been met with cooperation, repeated misbehavior, or abuse of time
- Rewards for a weekly clean streak is determined by parents
    - $1
- Three weeks of a clean streak can result in eligible celebratory event
    - Child presented with one or more available activities
- A length of the system duration and ending period bonus determined by parent
    - 3 months
- Child is provided full parameter space and agrees to participate in system
    - TBD


## Operations:
- Child determines how many units of time to risk as collateral for the upcoming week
- Units of time are taken into consideration by upcoming events for the Child and/or family
- The option of zero or any other whole number of time units can be selected by Child
- Parent collects collateral for selected amount of time units and updates ledger
- Each day the parent evaluates Child for any correctable behavior
- Reduction of collateral determined by Parent and ledger is updated for any changes
- On day of utilization, child is updated for remaining time units available and decides on how to spend their time from available activities
- Child keep track of their time and ends session when time is up
- Parent returns collateral on day of settlement
- Child determines the time units to risk for the upcoming week
- At the end of the agreed system duration, bonus is calculated and released to Child
